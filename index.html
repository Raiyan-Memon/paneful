FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature, theme and export size is free, with no watermark and no limits beyond the stores’ own (10 screenshots for the App Store, 8 for Google Play).

Do I need an account?

No. Open the editor and start. There’s nothing to sign up for.

Where are my screenshots stored?

In your browser’s own storage, on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. If you clear your browser data the project goes with it, so use “Save project file” in the editor’s menu to keep a backup.

Which sizes do I get?

Every size the stores ask for, each in its own folder in the ZIP.

Will Apple and Google accept these images?

They’re exported at the exact sizes each store asks for, as flat RGB PNGs with no transparency, which covers the usual reasons uploads get rejected. Run the pre-flight check before exporting to catch anything else.

Does it use AI?

No. Store preview, the thumbnail test, the pre-flight check and colour matching are all plain maths running in your browser, which is why it’s free and private.
