Tips for better App Store screenshots

  • Win the first three. Only the first few portrait screenshots show in search results. Put your strongest benefits there.
  • Benefits, not features. "Plan a week of meals in 5 minutes" beats "Meal planner screen".
  • Readable at a glance. Big type and short lines. If you have to squint at the thumbnail, it's too small.
  • Tell a story across slides. A consistent background and colour flow makes the row feel like one designed piece.
  • Use real screens. Apple's guidelines expect screenshots to show the app in use.

Questions

Which App Store screenshot sizes does Paneful export?

iPhone 6.9″ at 1320×2868, iPhone 6.5″ at 1284×2778 and iPad 13″ at 2064×2752, each in its own folder in the ZIP. App Store Connect scales these down for smaller devices.

Do I need screenshots for every iPhone size?

No. If your app looks the same across sizes, Apple only needs the largest required set and scales it down for the rest. Upload the 6.9″ set, plus the 13″ iPad set if your app runs on iPad.

Why does App Store Connect reject some screenshots?

The usual causes are a wrong pixel size or an alpha channel (transparency). Paneful exports exact sizes as flat RGB PNGs, which avoids both.

Is it free, and is there a watermark?

It is completely free with no watermark and no export limit. The App Store allows up to 10 screenshots per device size, so that is the only cap.

Do I need a Mac?

No. Paneful runs in any modern browser on Mac, Windows, Linux or a Chromebook.
