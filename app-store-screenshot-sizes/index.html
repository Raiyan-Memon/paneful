App Store screenshot sizes 2026.

Every iPhone and iPad screenshot size App Store Connect accepts, which ones you actually have to upload, and the file rules that cause most rejections.

Last checked against Apple's App Store Connect specifications: November 2026.

The short answer

Upload one iPhone set at 1320 × 2868 (6.9″). If your app runs on iPad, add one set at 2064 × 2752 (13″). App Store Connect scales those down for every smaller device. Use JPEG or PNG with no transparency, 1 to 10 screenshots per size.

That covers most apps. The tables below are for when you want to provide device-specific screenshots, or you're working from older captures.

File rules

  • Formats: .jpeg, .jpg or .png.
  • Count: at least 1 and up to 10 screenshots per device size.
  • No transparency: PNGs must not have an alpha channel.
  • Exact pixels: the dimensions must match one of the accepted sizes exactly. 1320 × 2869 will be refused.
  • App previews (videos) are separate and have their own specification. Up to three per size.

Why screenshots get rejected

"The dimensions of one or more screenshots are wrong"

The image is off by even a pixel, or you uploaded a 6.1″ capture into the 6.9″ slot. Resize to one of the exact sizes above, keeping the aspect ratio so the content doesn't stretch.

Alpha channel or transparency

Design tools often export PNGs with an alpha channel even when nothing is transparent. Re-export as a flat RGB PNG or a JPEG.

Content that doesn't match the app

Apple's review guidelines expect screenshots to show the app in use. Marketing headlines and frames are fine, but the screens themselves should be real.

Do your screenshots have to look like the device?

No. You can upload plain captures, framed devices, or designed slides with headlines. Most top apps use designed slides because the first three screenshots show in search results, and a headline explains the app faster than a raw screen.

Questions

What is the App Store screenshot size for iPhone in 2026?

The main iPhone size is the 6.9-inch display: 1320×2868 portrait (1290×2796 and 1260×2736 are also accepted). App Store Connect scales it down for smaller iPhones.

What is the iPad screenshot size for the App Store?

The 13-inch iPad size, 2064×2752 or 2048×2732 portrait, is required if your app runs on iPad. Smaller iPad sizes are scaled from it.

How many screenshots can I upload to the App Store?

At least 1 and up to 10 per device size, as JPEG or PNG.

Why does App Store Connect reject my screenshots?

Usually because the pixel dimensions don't exactly match an accepted size, or because the PNG has an alpha channel (transparency). Export at an exact size as a flat RGB PNG.

Do I need screenshots for every iPhone size?

No. If your app's interface is the same across sizes, provide the highest required resolution and App Store Connect scales it for the other displays.

Source: Apple, App Store Connect screenshot specifications. Apple updates these when new devices ship, so check there before a big launch.
