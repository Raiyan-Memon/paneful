When Paneful is the better fit

  • You don't want another subscription. You update screenshots a few times a year, and paying monthly for that doesn't make sense.
  • You want to start right now. There's no signup, onboarding or trial clock. Open the editor and drop in screenshots.
  • Privacy matters. Unreleased app screens never leave your device, which helps when you're working under NDA or before a launch.
  • You want guidance, not just a canvas. The store preview, thumbnail stand-out score and pre-flight check tell you whether the set will work before you upload.
  • You ship one or two languages. Writing a handful of headlines by hand is quick.

When AppScreens is the better fit

We'd rather you pick the right tool than the free one.

  • You localize into many languages. Automatic translation across dozens of locales saves real time, and Paneful doesn't have it.
  • You want uploads handled for you. Pushing straight to App Store Connect and Play Console removes a manual step at scale.
  • A team edits together. Cloud projects and shared workspaces suit agencies and larger teams.

Questions

Is there a free alternative to AppScreens?

Yes. Paneful is a free, browser-based App Store and Google Play screenshot maker with no account, no watermark and no export limit.

Can Paneful translate my screenshots?

Not automatically. You can duplicate a project and rewrite the headlines per language, but there is no built-in translation. If you ship in many languages, a tool with localization like AppScreens will save time.

Can Paneful upload to App Store Connect or Play Console?

No. Paneful gives you a ZIP with a folder per size, and you upload the files yourself.

Can I move my AppScreens project to Paneful?

There is no importer. Bring your raw app screenshots and headlines, pick a theme, and you can usually rebuild a set in a few minutes.
