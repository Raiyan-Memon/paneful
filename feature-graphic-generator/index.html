What the feature graphic is for

The feature graphic is the wide banner on your Google Play listing. Google uses it when your app is featured, in promotional spots, and above your listing on some devices. It is often the largest image a visitor sees for your app, which makes it worth more than a stretched logo on a flat colour.

Most feature graphic makers start from a blank template, so the banner ends up looking unrelated to the screenshots under it. Paneful builds the feature graphic from your first screenshot slide, so the colours, fonts and headline carry straight through.

Feature graphic requirements

RuleRequirement
Dimensions1024 × 500 px exactly
FormatJPEG or 24-bit PNG
TransparencyNot allowed (no alpha channel)

A PNG exported from a design tool with a transparent background is the most common reason Play Console refuses a feature graphic. Paneful's encoder always writes flat RGB, so this can't happen.

Design tips for a feature graphic that works

  • Say one thing. A headline of a few words is plenty. The banner is often shown small.
  • Keep important content away from the edges. Google may crop or overlay the banner in some placements, so leave breathing room.
  • Don't repeat your icon and name as the whole design. They already appear right next to the banner on the listing.
  • Match your screenshots. One colour story from the banner through the screenshots looks deliberate and trustworthy.
  • Check it on dark and light. Strong contrast inside the graphic keeps it readable on either.

Questions

What size is the Google Play feature graphic?

1024 px wide by 500 px tall, as a JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no alpha channel.

Why was my feature graphic rejected?

The most common reasons are the wrong dimensions or a PNG with transparency. Paneful exports exactly 1024×500 as a flat PNG with no alpha.

Is the feature graphic required?

Google lists it among the graphic assets for your store listing, and it is used when your app is featured and above your listing on some devices. It is worth making one even if you are not sure you will be featured.

How does Paneful design the feature graphic?

It uses your first slide's background and headline on the left and its device on the right, so the banner matches the rest of your screenshots. Edit slide 1 and the feature graphic updates with it.
