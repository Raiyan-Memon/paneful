Why your Play Store screenshots matter

On Google Play, most people decide whether to install from your icon, rating and the first few screenshots. They rarely read the description. Plain screenshots straight from the emulator show what the app looks like but not why someone should want it. A short headline, a clear device frame and a consistent colour story turn the same screens into an argument for your app.

Design tools like Figma can do this, but setting up Google's sizes, keeping eight slides consistent and exporting without transparency takes an afternoon. Paneful is built for exactly this one job, so it takes minutes.

Tips for Play Store screenshots that convert

  • Lead with your best feature. The first two or three screenshots are what most people see. Put the one thing your app does best first.
  • One idea per slide. A headline of three to six words, readable at thumbnail size.
  • Upload at least four screenshots. Google says apps need at least four screenshots of 1080 px or more to be eligible for some recommendation formats.
  • Keep it truthful. Show real screens from the current version. Google reviews store listing content.
  • Make the feature graphic count. It shows at the top of your listing and in promotions. Keep the key message away from the edges.

Questions

Is this Play Store screenshot generator really free?

Yes. Every theme, frame and export size is free, with no watermark and no export limit. Google Play itself allows up to 8 phone screenshots per listing, so that is the only cap.

What size are Google Play phone screenshots?

Paneful exports phone screenshots at 1080×1920 (9:16) and tablet screenshots at 1440×2560. Google accepts sides between 320 and 3,840 px, but asks for at least 1080 px for screenshots to be used in its recommendation formats.

Does it make the feature graphic too?

Yes. Tick Feature graphic in the export dialog and you get a 1024×500 image built from your first slide's background, headline and device, saved as a flat PNG with no transparency.

Will Google Play reject the images?

Exports are 24-bit PNGs with no alpha channel at sizes inside Google's limits, which covers the usual reasons uploads fail. Keep the content truthful to your app, since Google also reviews what the screenshots show.

Are my screenshots uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs and is stored in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
