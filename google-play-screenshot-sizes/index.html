Paneful / Google Play screenshot sizes

Google Play screenshot sizes 2026.

Phone, tablet, Chromebook, Wear OS and TV screenshot sizes, the feature graphic and app icon, plus the rules your screenshots need to meet to be featured.

Last checked against Google's Play Console Help: November 2026.

The short answer

Upload 4 to 8 phone screenshots at 1080 × 1920 (9:16), a 1024 × 500 feature graphic and a 512 × 512 icon. Use JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no transparency for screenshots and the feature graphic.

Phone screenshots

RuleRequirement
Count2 to 8 phone screenshots
DimensionsEach side between 320 px and 3,840 px. The long side can't be more than 2.3× the short side.
Aspect ratio9:16 portrait, 16:9 landscape, or both
FormatJPEG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha), up to 8 MB each
Recommended1080 × 1920 portrait or 1920 × 1080 landscape

For featuring: Google says apps need at least four screenshots with a minimum 1080 px resolution (9:16 at 1080×1920 or 16:9 at 1920×1080) to be eligible for recommendation formats that use screenshots. Games need at least three.

Tablet and Chromebook screenshots

RuleRequirement
CountAt least 4 for large screens, up to 8 per device type
DimensionsBetween 1,080 and 7,680 px
Aspect ratio16:9 landscape or 9:16 portrait
Common size1440 × 2560 portrait or 2560 × 1440 landscape

Google also advises keeping text inside the core app experience on large-screen screenshots, since anything near the edges can be cut off on Play's homepages.

Wear OS and Android TV

DeviceRequirement
Wear OSAt least 1 screenshot, 1:1 ratio, minimum 384 × 384. App interface only: no device frames, extra text, backgrounds or transparency.
Android TVAt least 1 TV screenshot to publish, plus a 1280 × 720 TV banner (JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no alpha).

Content rules worth knowing

  • Show the real app. Google asks for screenshots that show the actual in-app experience.
  • Device imagery: Google's guidance advises against device imagery and fingers tapping on devices. Many listings still use frames, but if you want to follow the guidance strictly, use bare screenshots with a headline instead.
  • Alt text: each screenshot can have alt text of up to 140 characters. Use it; it helps accessibility.
  • Requirements are enforced. Google says failing its listing requirements can lead to removal or suspension.

Questions

What is the best Google Play phone screenshot size?

1080×1920 (9:16 portrait). Google accepts sides from 320 to 3,840 px, but requires at least 1080 px for screenshots to be eligible for its recommendation formats.

How many screenshots does Google Play need?

Between 2 and 8 phone screenshots. Apps need at least 4 screenshots of 1080 px or more to be eligible for some recommendation formats.

What size is the Google Play feature graphic?

1024×500 px, as a JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no alpha channel.

What format should Google Play screenshots be?

JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no alpha channel, up to 8 MB each.

What size are Google Play tablet screenshots?

Sides between 1,080 and 7,680 px in a 16:9 or 9:16 ratio, with at least 4 screenshots for large screens. 1440×2560 portrait is a common choice.
